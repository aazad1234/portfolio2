    About me

    A disciplined eye for brand craft & visual form.

    I care deeply about how visual identities feel, how design carries emotional weight, and how print materials hold up in the physical world.

    For more than eight years I have crafted brand identities, bespoke publication grids, sustainable packaging, and digital art direction. That range bridges classic Swiss modernist graphic traditions with contemporary dynamic visual systems.

    I collaborate with founders, curators, and creative teams who value timeless design, thoughtful restraint, and tactile execution.

    Brand IdentityComprehensive visual marks and systems.
